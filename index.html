The Story Behind The Diff China

Built from hustle, honesty, and a leap of faith

6+ Years in China
Nzobo Kihouissinga Trésor Delmar
Co-Founder · The Diff China · Guangzhou
🇨🇬

Where I come from

I was born and raised in Congo Brazzaville in a modest, faith-filled family. Growing up as a servant at the Catholic Church shaped me early — discipline, humility, and a deep sense of purpose. My mother's words never left me: "To achieve anything in life, you must work hard and be honest." Those are the values I carry everywhere.

✈️

I packed my bags for China — 2019

I arrived in Suzhou to pursue a degree in Mechanical Engineering — alone, in a country with a completely different language and culture. I embraced the challenge, learned Mandarin to Level 5, and built my life from scratch, one day at a time.

🎯

Guangzhou — where I found my calling

Before finishing my degree, I moved to Guangzhou and worked part-time as a model. Out in the city every day, I kept getting approached by foreigners lost in the markets, asking me to help them translate. I realized I was already doing this naturally — and that people genuinely needed it.

💡

The hardest decision I ever made

I quit my modeling job to go all-in on my sourcing business — with school fees still unpaid and no guaranteed income. It was scary. But I trusted that if I worked hard and stayed honest, nothing would be impossible. That trust is what kept me going.

🌍

Today — and I'm just getting started

The Diff China now serves buyers across Africa, Europe, and beyond — with a 220,000-strong online community, major factory partnerships in Guangzhou, and a reputation built on the same two values my mother taught me: hard work and honesty.

"Work hard and be honest." — These aren't marketing words. They are the foundation this business was built on, from Brazzaville to Guangzhou.
